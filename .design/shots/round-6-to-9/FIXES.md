# Rounds 6 to 9

## Round 6 (blind critic: 5/10)

The critic's hits and what was done:

1. The viewport sat in a bezel with the stripe along its bottom; it read as a generic framed screenshot. Fix: the frame is now the slate itself. The clapper stripe is the bar across its top and hangs past the left edge the way the sticks hang past a slate; the shot fields (lens, shot count, eyeline) are written along its bottom edge in condensed capitals, the way a slate carries its fields on its face.
2. A control panel floating inside the viewport, on top of the scene. Fix: the panel belongs to the headset only; the page has its own lens row, Snap frame and Reset stage under the frame, wired to the same signals.
3. Flat, unlit figures on a flat plate. Fix: a warm key light with a 1024 shadow map, cast and receive shadows on every piece, a wood grain texture on the lay figures and a seam on the tennis ball.
4. The camera too high, looking down at a model. Fix: table-eye height, looking across the set at the monitor.
5. A three-fact block under the button. Fix: removed; the lede carries the one sentence that matters.
6. Light theme pasted the dark bezel onto paper. Fix: on paper the frame is drawn, a pencil-weight outline around the screen.
7. Wordmark too large for a header. Fix: 136 px.
