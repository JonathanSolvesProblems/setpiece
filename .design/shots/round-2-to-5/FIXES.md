## Round 2 (blind critic: 6/10 pages, 3/10 scene)

Critic's hits and what was done:

1. Standard text-left, image-right hero. Fix: the live stage in its bezel became the hero's one object; the generated drawing left the first screen (kept for the OG image and the 404).
2. Dark theme had an empty right half. Cause: capture timing right after a restart; verified the image loads, then the composition change made it moot.
3. The drawing's generated tells (a ring on the camera, a clapper on a mic stand). Fix: off the first screen, as above.
4. One condensed face for body, button, helper and values. Fix: Schibsted Grotesk for body on the page and for the values, status, shot rows and buttons in the panel; Big Shoulders kept for labels and lens numbers; the stencil kept for the headline, the wordmark and the slate strip.
5. Column edges disagreeing (headline 420, stripe 405, paragraph 380). Fix: one copy width variable (400 px) for headline, stripe and copy.
6. Top-right tagline breaking badly. Fix: removed; the competition line lives in the footer.
7. Copy in fragments. Fix: the lede rewritten as two sentences.
8. The 3D scene captured with emulator chrome and the panel cropped. Fix: captures are taken from the page frame with the session exited; the emulator's XR overlay is not the judged surface.

4. Stencil at 10 to 12 px smears. Fix: Big Shoulders (not stencil) for every label under display size, on the page and in the panel.
