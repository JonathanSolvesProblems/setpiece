# Found details

Reference photographs in this folder (licences in credits.json). Observations are tied to a file. Hex values come from `eyedrop.py` on that file unless marked as a domain fact.

## The slate (clapperboard-0.jpg, clapperboard-1.jpg, clapperboard-2.jpg, a German Filmklappe from 1992)

1. The board is matte black paint, dusted and smudged with old chalk. Under daylight the painted surface reads #3f4044 (clapperboard-1) and its shadowed side #1e232b (clapperboard-0). The chalk haze sits on it as a mid warm grey, #6f6c6b.
2. Three field labels run down the left edge, white stencil capitals on a painted strip: REGIE, KAMERA, TON. The values are written beside them by hand in chalk. Label left in stencil, value right in a hand: that is the slate's whole information design.
3. The title GEDANKEN is hand-lettered in chalk across the top in larger capitals. One loud line, everything else small.
4. The clapper sticks are two wooden bars with alternating black and white diagonal stripes, paint worn through to bare wood at the corners, and a stencilled code FW36 on the face of the stick.
5. The sticks hang past the right edge of the board. The silhouette is a rectangle with a striped bar that overshoots.
6. Beside the slate lies the camera report: a printed cream form with field captions (Titel, Rollen-Nummer, Datum), filled in with blue ballpoint ("Premiere", "24.4.92"), folded once.

## The lay figures (artist-lay-figure-wooden-mannequin-0.jpg)

7. Two wooden artist's mannequins on a shelf among cables and a speaker: light wood #d9ac8b with highlights #eec7a9, mid tone #a37a60, joints and shadows #64453e and #433531. Round heads, no faces, ball joints, each standing on a round black base on a post.
8. The background behind them is a warm near-black, #1e1616. It is a workshop shelf, not a studio backdrop.

## The storyboard page (storyboard-film-drawing-0.jpg)

9. Aged paper, #f5f6e5 to #f6f7ef, with pencil frames drawn freehand in a three-wide grid, borders uneven, each frame numbered. Pencil grey #87877d. One frame carries a red pencil mark. Notes sit in the margin in the same pencil.

## The crew (camera-assistant-focus-puller-film-set-0.jpg and -1.jpg, Owen Roizman on The Exorcist, 1973)

10. A 35 mm camera on a dolly: magazine on top, matte box in front, tape and stickers on the body, chrome and black. The crew huddles around the eyepiece. Everything on a working camera is labelled with tape.

## The drawn clapper (clapperboard-3.jpg)

11. A charcoal sketch of a clapperboard with "Action!" under it: uneven line weight, smudged fill in the stripes, no straight edges. A hand drawing of the object, not a diagram.

## Domain facts (not from the photos)

12. On a VFX set the creature is not there. Its eyeline is a tennis ball on a C-stand raised to the creature's eye height, so the actor has something to look at. The ball is optic yellow, the ITF colour for tennis balls.
13. A red tally light on the camera means it is recording.
14. Lenses live in a foam case with each focal length written on tape: 24, 35, 50, 85.
15. The 1st AC labels everything with white paper camera tape and a black marker.
