# Voice

**Who speaks:** a first assistant camera who has slated a thousand takes, talking to a director sitting at the table. Short, exact, a little dry. Says the lens number before the adjective.

## Five lines

- Headline: Block the shot on your kitchen table
- Button: Snap frame
- Empty state: No frames yet. Move the camera, then snap.
- Error: The table did not scan. Grab the plate and set it down where you want it.
- Confirmation: Board sent. Six frames, three lenses, two eyelines.

## Banned

immersive, seamless, experience (as a noun for the product), intuitive, powerful, next-generation, unlock, elevate, effortless, revolutionary.

## Vocabulary

Reader's words, used on every surface: shot, take, lens, frame, board, shot list, eyeline, mark, slate, plate, stand-in, camera height, distance.

Internal words that never appear on a product surface: entity, component, anchor, session, raycast, passthrough, WebXR, scene understanding, mesh. They belong in the README and nowhere a filmmaker reads.

## Rules kept from the kit

Sentence case everywhere. No long dashes. No negative parallelism. No rule of three by reflex. Numbers as digits with their unit: 35 mm, 1.2 m, 28 deg. A missing number is a word.
