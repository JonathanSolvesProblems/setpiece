Voice

A first assistant camera who has slated a thousand takes, talking to a director at the table.

Block the shot on your kitchen table. / Snap frame. / No frames yet. Move the camera, then snap. / The table did not scan. Grab the plate and set it down where you want it. / Board sent. Six frames, three lenses, two eyelines.

Charter, the short form

  1. Chalk is the only text colour on slate. Yellow and red are never text.
  2. Titles carry no full stop and no comma.
  3. One loud stencil line per screen; stencil otherwise only for field labels and frame numbers.
  4. A field is a stencil label left and a value right, on one line.
  5. The clapper stripe appears exactly once per screen.
  6. Nothing on the stage pretends to be the finished image.
  7. Optic yellow marks the eyeline ball, the selected lens and a stage that is good to go.
  8. Tally red only while something records.
  9. The monitor is the only bordered object.
  10. Chalk haze on every slate surface, never over text.
  11. One filled button per screen.
  12. Numbers in fixed columns with fixed precision; a missing value is a word.
