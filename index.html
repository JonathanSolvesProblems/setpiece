Block the shot on
your kitchen table

A 1:10 film set on a slate, placed on your own table. You move wooden stand-ins and a 35 mm camera with your hands, look through the lens, snap, and leave with the storyboard and the shot list.

Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser, sit at a table, and the set lands on it.

Try it here first: drag a figure, pick a lens, snap a frame. The eyeline from each actor to the creature's eyes updates as you move them, in degrees and in metres on the real set.
